# Pilot landing-page copy

## Shared framing

**Headline:** A clearer pre-session brief for traders who do not have time to assemble every signal.

**Body:** Select an instrument, provide the market inputs you already use, and receive a concise brief covering evidence, scenarios, invalidation conditions, and risks. The terminal does not execute trades or promise profits.

**Primary action:** Try the EUR/JPY workflow

**Secondary action:** Join the pilot interview list

**Trust note:** This is an early validation pilot. Your feedback helps us decide whether the workflow is useful; it is not investment advice.

## Variant A — EUR/JPY intraday

**Audience line:** For traders planning EUR/JPY sessions around macro, geopolitical, and technical risk.

**Prompt:** What would you want checked before your next EUR/JPY session?

**Offer:** See the prototype workflow and tell us what is missing.

## Variant B — part-time multi-asset

**Audience line:** For traders who review several markets but cannot monitor them continuously.

**Prompt:** Which instrument would you review first if one brief gathered the context for you?

**Offer:** Compare one forex workflow with a stock/index or commodities/crypto workflow.

## Variant C — stock/index

**Audience line:** For stock and index traders who want earnings, macro, news, and risk context in one place.

**Prompt:** What usually takes the most manual work before a session?

**Offer:** Join a short workflow test; no broker connection is required.

## Variant D — commodities/crypto

**Audience line:** For traders who need headline monitoring and explicit volatility warnings.

**Prompt:** Which missing risk would make you reject a market scenario?

**Offer:** Review a sample brief and challenge its assumptions.

## Qualification fields

Ask only after the person chooses to continue:

- primary asset category;
- first instrument they would test;
- sessions traded per week;
- whether they currently pay for a charting, research, alert, or journal product;
- preferred interview contact method, collected only with explicit permission.

Do not collect broker credentials, account identifiers, trading account balances, or personal financial details.
