# Niche brief — trading intelligence terminal

**Decision date:** 2026-11-05. **Default geography:** Spain/EU. **Status:** trading evidence rebuild; the Day 9 habit-app numbers are a class-method demo, not trading evidence.

## Product and decision

A browser trading assistant for discretionary retail traders: the user supplies calendar screenshots, a chart, optional OHLCV CSV and context; the product returns a concise, instrument-aware brief with scenarios, invalidation conditions and risks. EUR/JPY is the first workflow; stock/index, commodities/crypto and part-time multi-asset are comparison niches.

Choose the first niche, monthly price test and acquisition route. Do not launch if the trading pilot cannot show qualified brief activation, repeat use, and credible paid intent. The old €500/P10 and conversion thresholds remain **assumptions**, not evidence.
