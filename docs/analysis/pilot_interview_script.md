# Pilot interview script

## Purpose

Learn whether active traders experience the target workflow problem and whether the prototype changes their planning process. Do not lead participants toward a positive answer.

## Consent and privacy opening

“We are testing an early workflow, not evaluating your trading performance and not giving investment advice. Participation is voluntary. You may skip any question or stop at any time. We will record notes using a study ID, not your name. Please do not share broker credentials, account numbers, balances, or private documents. May we continue?”

Record:

- study ID;
- consent given: yes/no;
- date and asset category;
- note-taking permission: yes/no.

Do not record audio or collect contact details unless the participant separately agrees to it.

## Context questions

1. What markets or instruments do you actively review?
2. Walk me through your last pre-session or trade-planning routine.
3. Which tools or sources do you open, and in what order?
4. Where does the process slow down or become uncertain?
5. Tell me about the last time you missed or discovered a relevant catalyst late.
6. What do you currently pay for, if anything, in this workflow?

Use “What happened next?” and “Can you show me the step?” before asking “Would you use this?”

## Prototype task

Give the participant one workflow and do not explain the expected answer:

1. Select the instrument you would review first.
2. Provide only the inputs you would normally use.
3. Request the brief.
4. Find the strongest scenario and its invalidation condition.
5. Find the source or input context behind one important claim.
6. Ask one follow-up question or challenge one assumption.
7. State what, if anything, you would do differently in your real workflow.

Record actions and observations, not just opinions.

## Debrief questions

1. What part was most useful?
2. What part was unclear or untrustworthy?
3. What information was missing?
4. What would stop you from using this in a real planning session?
5. How often would this workflow be relevant?
6. What existing tool would this replace, complement, or fail to replace?
7. Which of these would you prefer: free trial, monthly plan, one-off report, or none?
8. What would you need to see before paying?

## Qualification rule

Classify an interview as qualified only when the participant:

- actively reviews or trades the selected market;
- describes a recent real workflow;
- completes the prototype task;
- identifies a repeated problem or explicitly says it is absent.

Do not classify enthusiasm, hypothetical interest, or a request for investment advice as validation.

## Close

Ask whether the participant wants to be contacted for a later pilot. Store contact details separately from study notes and only with explicit permission.
