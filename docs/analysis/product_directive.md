# Product directive — trading intelligence terminal

The terminal is for time-constrained part-time and multi-asset traders. It turns user-supplied market inputs into a concise, instrument-aware decision and risk brief so they can plan a session without manually assembling several tools. It must preserve the distinction between evidence, interpretation, and uncertainty. Done for the validation pilot means that a real participant can complete the EUR/JPY workflow, understand the resulting scenarios and invalidation conditions, and provide a measurable usefulness and follow-up signal.

## Invariants

1. Every brief is explicitly tied to the selected instrument and analysis time.
2. Every material claim has source or input context; missing data is stated rather than invented.
3. Every scenario includes conditions that would invalidate it and a clear risk warning.
4. The terminal supports the trader's decision; it never presents an output as a guaranteed trade or executes a trade.
5. The user controls which inputs are submitted and can inspect or challenge the brief through follow-up questions.

## Human-owned decisions

This directive is a working pilot default. Changing the target segment, promise, or invariants requires a dated decision entry outside an experiment wave.
